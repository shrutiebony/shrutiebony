# 🌟 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer specializing in Distributed Systems, Backend Infrastructure, and AI Systems** with **3+ years of industry experience** building scalable cloud-native platforms, enterprise microservices, knowledge graph systems, and agentic AI applications.

I enjoy solving engineering problems that require systems to **process, connect, and reason over large-scale data**.

Currently, I'm a **Research Assistant at Rutgers University–New Brunswick**, where I'm building a distributed graph intelligence platform for large-scale public data. The system uses Kafka-based ingestion pipelines, distributed document processing, MongoDB, Neo4j knowledge graphs, NetworkX graph analytics, and FastAPI services to transform unstructured information into searchable graph-based intelligence.

Previously, I interned as a **Software Development Engineer II at Cisco Webex**, where I designed and implemented an **agentic AI platform using the Model Context Protocol (MCP)**. I built LLM-driven systems capable of dynamically discovering, registering, and invoking tools at runtime through multi-agent reasoning, parallel execution, and autonomous decision-making.

Before that, I spent over three years at **BT Group**, developing enterprise-scale backend systems and cloud-native microservices. My work included modernizing monolithic applications into distributed architectures, implementing event-driven systems with Apache Kafka, building telecom knowledge graph solutions with Neo4j, deploying services on AWS, and optimizing large-scale data pipelines from **22.5 hours to just 40 minutes**.

Outside of work I love hiking, camping and food!
